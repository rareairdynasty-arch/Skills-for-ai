Questions

Do I need to bring my car anywhere?

No. Rare Air Details is fully mobile. We come to your home, office or wherever your car is parked in San Diego and surrounding areas.

How do I book?

Call or text 858-519-6591, or send a message to @rareairdetails on Instagram. Tell us your car, the package you want and where it will be, and we'll set a time.

Which package should I choose?

Simple keeps a well-kept car clean. Simple + is for when the inside needs work too. All Out is the complete detail, including the engine bay and a coat of wax. If you're unsure, call and describe your car and we'll recommend one.

Can you do something that isn't in a package?

Call or text and tell us what you need. We'll talk through what's possible and the price with you.
